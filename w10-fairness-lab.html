You split $10 with five responders. Each has inequality-averse preferences u = x − α·max(y − x, 0): they dislike getting less than you. One is a pure egoist. Make each an offer, then guess who is who.

Everyone picks 0 to 100. The winner is closest to p × the average. Set the share of players at each level of reasoning and see what wins (Angner 11.4; Nagel 1995).
