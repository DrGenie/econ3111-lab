10,000 simulated students decide whether to get a free flu vaccine on campus. Each has a benefit, a hassle cost, present bias and limited attention. Change the choice architecture and watch uptake. Nothing here bans or pays anyone: these are nudges and sludge.

Choice architecture




Model: a student gets vaccinated if they notice, and benefit × β exceeds today's hassle cost. Defaults remove the need to notice; reminders raise attention; norms raise perceived benefit; plans reduce forgetting. Parameters are illustrative, not estimates.
