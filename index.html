This online laboratory accompanies ECON3111 Behavioural Economics. Each week has interactive simulations, experiments and short quizzes that apply the concepts in the slides. Open the page for the current week by scanning the QR code on the slides, or choose it below. Every page runs in your browser and works on a phone. Nothing you enter is sent to a server.
